# Vendra Return and Cancellation Policy

Welcome to Vendra! We strive to make your shoe shopping experience as smooth as possible. Below are the rules governing order cancellation, returns, and refunds.

## 1. Order Cancellation and Refunds
- **Cancellation Window:** Customers can cancel and receive a full refund for any order within **7 days** of the purchase date, provided the items are unworn and in their original packaging.
- **After 7 Days:** Orders cancelled or returned after the 7-day window are eligible for **store credit** only. Store credit does not expire and can be used on any future purchase.
- **Sale Items:** Items purchased on sale, clearance, or during promotional discount events are marked as **Final Sale** and are non-refundable and non-exchangeable.
- **Condition of Items:** To qualify for any refund or store credit, shoes must be completely unworn, clean, and in their original box with all tags intact. We reserve the right to inspect returned items and refuse refunds for items showing visible wear.
- **Refund Method:** Approved refunds will be credited back to the original method of payment (e.g., Stripe/credit card) within 5–10 business days of processing.
